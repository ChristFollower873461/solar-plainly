# Code of Conduct

Solar Plainly contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Protect private homeowner and contributor information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, especially solar industry sales or financing relationships.
- Correct mistakes openly and without retaliation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Knowingly introducing misleading consumer claims.
- Using project channels for unsolicited sales or lead generation.
- Disrupting discussion or review in bad faith.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or access when necessary to protect the community.
